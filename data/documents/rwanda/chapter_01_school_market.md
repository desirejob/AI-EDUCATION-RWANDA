# Rwanda learning context — School market percentages

**Source status:** Locally authored fictional exercise; not REB material and not a real market-price report.

A fictional school-market notebook costs 5,000 RWF. Its price is increased by 20%. Calculate the increase and the new price.

`increase = (20 / 100) × 5,000 = 1,000 RWF`

`new price = 5,000 + 1,000 = 6,000 RWF`

| Item | Original price (RWF) | Increase | New price (RWF) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Notebook (fictional) | 5,000 | 20% | 6,000 |

All prices in this exercise are invented for learning. Do not use them as current local prices.

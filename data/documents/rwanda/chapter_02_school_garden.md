# Rwanda learning context — School garden ratios

**Source status:** Locally authored fictional classroom scenario; not an official agriculture report.

A fictional school garden has 12 tomato seedlings and 8 bean seedlings. The ratio of tomato to bean seedlings is `12:8`. Divide both numbers by their greatest common divisor, 4: `12:8 = 3:2`.

| Seedling | Count in fictional example |
|---|---:|
| Tomato | 12 |
| Bean | 8 |

Ratios compare quantities in the same order. Reversing the order gives bean to tomato as `2:3`.

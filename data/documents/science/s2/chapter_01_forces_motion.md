# S2 Science — Forces and motion

**Source status:** Umuseke learning note, not an REB textbook. Internal page 1.

A force is a push or pull. Forces can change an object's speed, direction, or shape. The SI unit of force is the newton (N).

For a constant force acting on an object, Newton's second law is `F = m × a`, where `F` is force in newtons, `m` is mass in kilograms, and `a` is acceleration in metres per second squared.

**Worked example:** A 2 kg cart accelerates at 3 m/s². `F = 2 × 3 = 6 N`.

| Quantity | Symbol | SI unit |
|---|---|---|
| Force | F | newton (N) |
| Mass | m | kilogram (kg) |
| Acceleration | a | m/s² |

Friction acts against relative motion between surfaces. Ask which forces act on an object before predicting its motion.

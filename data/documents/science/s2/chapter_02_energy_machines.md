# S2 Science — Energy and simple machines

**Source status:** Umuseke learning note, not an REB textbook. Internal page 2.

Energy is the capacity to cause change. It can be transferred between stores, for example from chemical energy in a battery to electrical energy and then light and thermal energy in a lamp.

For work done by a constant force in the direction of motion, `W = F × d`. Work is measured in joules (J), force in newtons (N), and distance in metres (m).

**Worked example:** A learner pushes a box with 10 N over 2 m. `W = 10 × 2 = 20 J`.

| Simple machine | Example | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| Lever | Crowbar | Direction or size of force |
| Pulley | Flagpole pulley | Direction of force |
| Inclined plane | Ramp | Force needed over a longer distance |

A machine can make a task easier by changing force or direction; it does not create energy.

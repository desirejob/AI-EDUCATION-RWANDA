# S2 Science — Matter and mixtures

**Source status:** Umuseke learning note, not an REB textbook. Internal page 3.

Matter has mass and occupies space. A pure substance has a fixed composition; a mixture contains substances physically combined. Mixtures may be separated by physical methods because their components retain their properties.

| Method | Useful property | Example mixture
|---|---|
| Filtration | Insoluble solid particles are larger than pores | Sand and water |
| Evaporation | Solvent evaporates; dissolved solid remains | Salt solution |
| Decanting | Different settling rates or layers | Muddy water after settling |

In a fair separation, identify the properties of the components first. Evaporation can recover a dissolved solid, while distillation can also collect the condensed solvent.
